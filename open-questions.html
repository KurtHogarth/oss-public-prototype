What is settled, what is not

Not hiding trade-offs is good faith.

This page is Principle 5.0 in action: the system evaluates decisions by working through scenarios and precedents, not by asserting them as self-evident. The geolibertarian frame below remains one deliberate choice among alternatives, not the only possible one (see Core).

Closed with rationale

Not “we thought about it and decided” — every item below went through a review of a concrete precedent or scenario. The link is to the source section.

§4

Leverage-module "systemic significance" thresholds

Relative (% of local market), not absolute figures — an absolute threshold has a different effect in different jurisdictions.

§2.5

Sunset clause for the arbiter's temporary powers

Automatic expiry after 2 years, burden of proof on renewal (qualified majority + public justification), modeled on South Korea's Sunset for Review.

§3.9

Sovereign Fund governance

Four-tier structure (Strategist / Operator / independent Ethics body / Executive council, modeled on GPFG) — with an amendment: suspending the Ethics body requires the Amendments threshold (2/3 + 90 days), not a simple majority.

Precedent (independently confirmed): on 7 November 2025 the Norwegian Storting, by an ordinary majority (85 to 17, no qualified threshold), suspended the GPFG Council on Ethics' power to publicly recommend company exclusions — the Council now passes unpublished risk assessments to Norges Bank instead. The final new framework is due from a dedicated committee by 15 October 2026 — not ready as of writing; the case is still unfolding, not closed. Sources: Norwegian Ministry of Finance, European Pensions.

§7.1

Agentic system architecture

A hierarchical pattern with domain-separated supervisors, not one central supervisor — that would conflict with Art. VI. Connectivity via open standards A2A (Linux Foundation) + MCP.

§8

International legal status of the first fork

Three amendments following a review of Próspera ZEDE (Honduras, an ongoing $11B dispute in 2022–2026, including the removal of Constitutional Chamber judges): parallel negotiations in 2–3 jurisdictions, mandatory benefit-sharing with the local community, an independent arbitration clause agreed in advance.

§8.1

Connectivity between forks after a split

A voluntary layer: a forkable technical commons, portable reputation only by agreement, "one fork — one vote", protection against quiet standard hegemony — a special-cause audit and auto-suspension of a disputed practice.

§7.4.6

Influence transparency for public representatives

Threshold disclosure by reach, not by office, with a presumption of non-disclosure and judicial review of its application. Coordination cluster detection via a continuous 4-indicator score instead of a gameable discrete rule.

§2.4.2

Quorum for Amendments votes

The base 2/3 threshold is confirmed as workable. Decision: mandatory turnout transparency without a hard quorum, plus an emergency 72-hour path only for TECHNICAL fixes, auto-expiring after 90 days.

Synthesis with an alternative document

A third-party architecture document with overlapping vision was reviewed in full — what was accepted, what was rejected, and one case that deserves separate attention.

Accepted and integrated

  • VSM (Viable System Model, Stafford Beer) — a rigorous functional frame for the level ladder house→…→state, tested over decades (the Cybersyn project, Chile, 1970s)
  • Graceful Degradation — a precise formalization of the fork right and anti-concentration already built in (Art. VI, IX)
  • Consent-based decision-making (sociocracy) — for micro-decisions at "House" scale (Dunbar's number)
  • DID + soulbound tokens — an independent convergence with the already-designed Genesis consent
  • Civic Scaffolding — integrated into 7.4

Rejected with rationale

  • Thermodynamic/emergy economics — effectively repeats resource-based economics, already rejected as premature
  • Compute as a currency anchor — structurally unfit: no fixed supply, deflationary by Moore's law, a documented "inflationary tokenomics → death spiral" failure mode in real DePIN projects
Red flag · source check

"Universal law of game theory" and the Resilience = M × E × C formula

The document put a "universal theory," presented as academic, at the center of its analysis. A provenance check found: the source is not peer-reviewed scholarship but original content from an independent online geopolitics podcast; part of the theory rests on reading religious eschatological prophecy as a predictor of current politics — speculative commentary, not an institution-design tool.

The formula was run through a blind comparison against a real benchmark — the 4R framework by Bruneau et al. (MCEER, 2003), used by FEMA and the EU Civil Protection Mechanism. Verdict: as a literal formula it is useless (factors without units, multiplying unlike quantities is a metaphor, not an equation); the multiplicative intuition is real, but it belongs to Liebig's law of the minimum, not to this document's author.

Decision: the theory is not built into OSS architecture. The existing game-theory base (Prisoner's Dilemma, Stag Hunt, Chicken Game) remains the working foundation. Under Art. IX the alternative document may develop as its own fork with a different economic model — the shared Core (Art. I–XI) is compatible with both versions.

METRIC_GAMING · 7.4.6

Distributed evasion of disclosure thresholds

An influence coordinator may spread presence across all four indicators while staying under each threshold — continuous scoring reduces but does not eliminate the risk.

CUSTODY · 2.8 / 3.13

Hidden oral inheritance arrangements

Formal records cannot easily detect oral nominee arrangements — a limit of any formal audit, not an OSS-only bug.

STANDARD DRIFT · 8.1

Quiet standard hegemony between audits

Protection is periodic (every 2 years plus ad-hoc audits), not continuous — drift can appear between audits and be found after the fact.
