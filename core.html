Constitutional core · OSS_Core_Articles

Eleven articles

Short form for a reader from the street. Canonical signing text is in Core Articles; here — meaning and why.

The Core changes only by a super-strict procedure (Art. X). Economic overlay (LVT, etc.) is a deliberate choice; a fork with another model stays Core-compatible if it keeps I–XI.

I–III · Person, land, freedom

I · Self-ownership / NAP

Ban on aggression against person and lawfully acquired property. LVT is an explicit land exception, not a tax on labor.

II · Property and LVT

Property and land-tax rules are stated openly, without hidden “social need” extractions through vague norms.

III · Presumption of Freedom

Everything not expressly forbidden is allowed. Ambiguity is resolved in favor of freedom of action.

IV–V · Mobilization and anti-cult

IV · Ban on mandatory mobilization

Military and other forced service are outside the law. Defense is voluntary contract and incentives.

V · Anti-cult

Institutions are not built around a leader, savior-party, or irreplaceable symbol of power.

VI–VII · Anti-concentration and transparency

Visually: three spheres without a “CEO of the system”.

Enforcement

decide · enforce · court · conscientious refusal

Data

read · score · recommend · not policy

Finance

custody · allocate · LVT · Fund

VI · One sphere per institute

Court, citizen archive, and treasury cannot share one institutional contour. Crossings only via explicit boundaries.

VII · Reciprocal transparency

Who applies surveillance or coercion to people accepts symmetric observability of the institute. Principle 5.0: behavior aggregates, not a label on a person.

VIII–XI · Entry, exit, change, equality

VIII · Genesis

Consent to the Core (18+) opens voice and roles. Baseline rights and dividend logic are not sold for loyalty.

IX · Right of exit

Fork or relocate without losing personhood or lawful assets. A condition of legitimacy, not an emergency mode.

X · Amendment procedure

The Core is not a “chat post”. Strict procedure; Extensions have separate thresholds.

XI · Equality before the law

No estate immunity. One procedure for strong and weak nodes of the system.
