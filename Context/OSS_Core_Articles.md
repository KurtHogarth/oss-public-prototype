# Operating System for Society (OSS)
## Constitutional Core — Articles I–XI

> **Document status**: Canonical edition extracted from the full White Paper + PRD (`OSS_WP_PRD.md`) for separate signing via Genesis Consent (Article VIII). This file contains only the normative text — without justifications, precedents, or discussion, which remain in the main document. In case of discrepancy between this file and the main WP, the main WP is the source of revision history; this file is updated manually thereafter and is not a separate decision branch.
>

### Preamble

People are the source of legitimacy of any form of coordination among them. This Core establishes the non-derogable conditions of existence of the OSS and does not create the listed rights, but recognizes them as belonging to a person by the fact of their existence.

---

### Article I — Self-Ownership and the Non-Aggression Principle

Every person exclusively owns their own body, time, and the fruits of their labor. No one has the right to initiate aggression — physical coercion, threat, or deception leading to coercion — against the person or property of another, except for proportionate defense against such aggression.

### Article II — Inviolability of Property and Status of Common Resources

Property acquired without violation of Article I is inviolable and cannot be taken without the voluntary consent of the owner, with one explicit exception: **common resources** (land, subsurface resources, spectrum, and other objects with inelastic supply not created by the owner's labor) are not the product of anyone's labor, and the rent on them (Land Value Tax) is not a tax on property and not a violation of this Article, but payment for exclusive use of that which by its nature belongs to all in equal measure. This exception is formulated narrowly and cannot be expanded by interpretation to other kinds of property.

### Article III — Presumption of Freedom

Everything that is not expressly prohibited by this Core as a violation of the rights of another person is permitted. Any uncertainty is interpreted in favor of freedom of action, not in favor of the authority to restrict it.

### Article IV — Prohibition of Forced Mobilization

No one may be compelled to participate in armed forces, labor duty, or any other obligatory service; any defense of the OSS is exclusively on the basis of voluntary contract. This does not apply to the performance of roles to which a person has already given Genesis Consent (Article VIII) — including lottery arbitration — provided that such a role may be refused at any time without loss of basic rights, with the sole consequence of temporary exclusion from the pool for that specific function.

### Article V — Peaceful Transfer of Authority and Prohibition of Personality Cult

Any delegated power is temporary, accountable, and transferred peacefully. Elevation of a specific person as the source of legitimacy of the system is prohibited — legitimacy belongs to the principles of the Core, not to a person, institution, or office.

### Article VI — Anti-Concentration of Power

No institution of the OSS, including the AI arbiter, may simultaneously control more than one of the three spheres: (a) law enforcement, (b) collection of data on citizens, (c) financial flows. Separation is ensured technically (structural isolation of systems), not declaratively.

### Article VII — Principle of Reciprocal Transparency (Palantir Principle)

Any actor — state or private — that deploys a system of mass surveillance, profiling, or coercion over people thereby acknowledges the legitimacy of applying an analogous instrument to the persons who take decisions on such application (not to rank-and-file executors). Reciprocity applies to the **beneficial initiator** who exercises substantive control over the decision to apply surveillance in the concrete case, and not only to the person by whose hands it is physically performed — including the client if the executor was hired specifically to conceal that control. The distinction is not by office, but by the presence of agency in the decision to apply the instrument in the given case: a rank-and-file executor without the right to decide against whom and for what purpose the instrument is applied is protected; a person hired specifically to conceal the true client is not.

### Article VIII — Genesis Consent

Rights under Articles I–VII belong to a person by virtue of their existence and are never conditioned on consent — including a share in common resources (Citizen's Dividend), which is also due to minors. The right to vote, the right to occupy a delegated role, and the duties associated therewith (including LVT as a landowner) arise only upon voluntary cryptographically confirmed consent of an adult person and may be revoked at any time by the right of exit (Article IX) without loss of personal assets. The age of majority and the scope of legal responsibility flowing therefrom are uniform for all people regardless of sex characteristics — without exceptions.

### Article IX — Right of Exit

Any person or community has the right to exit from under the operation of a concrete implementation of the OSS (fork, relocation) without loss of personal rights or assets attached to the person, not to the jurisdiction.

### Article X — Amendment Procedure

1. This Core (Articles I–XI) is amended only by unanimous decision of all active forks of the OSS after no less than one year of public discussion.
2. Derivative norms (Extensions) are amended by thresholds differentiated according to the nature of the decision — never by simple majority without prior public discussion.
3. Any temporary expansion of powers of any institution (Transitional Powers) operates for no longer than a fixed term and expires automatically if not extended by qualified majority with public justification of necessity.

### Article XI — Equality Before the Law and Prohibition of Procedural Immunity

The holder of any delegated role, regardless of level, is subject to the same standards of application of the law — including detention and use of force when grounds exist — as any other person. No office, rank, or role creates a special permissive threshold for actions against it by any holder of law-enforcement powers. Any such action is subject to mandatory independent review within 24–48 hours, with personal accountability of the initiator through the rating system in case of lack of justification.

---

### Principle 5.0 — Observed Behavior, Not Predicted Predisposition

*(included here as an interpretive principle for the application of Articles VI, VII, and XI — not a separate article, but the frame in which they are read.)*

The Core and the accountability mechanisms derived from it do not evaluate personality, intention, or psychological profile of a person before they have committed a verifiable action. Limitation of power is achieved structurally (Article VI) and through visibility of past and present behavior over time (Articles VII, XI), and not through selection of the "right" people at the entrance or judgment of their character.
