Homelessness protection — three lines

Explicit boundary: LVT may lead to a plot changing owner through sale or a lien after death, but cannot be grounds for evicting a living person.

LINE 1

Dividend auto-offset

By default, no application needed. A landholder at or below the average value effectively pays nothing or is a net receiver.

LINE 2

Deferral with lien

For elderly/income-loss cases — the tax accrues as a lien at 3–6%/year, no eviction, recovered at sale/inheritance. Capped at 80% equity.

LINE 3

Emergency bridge

Short-term, interest-free loan (3–12 months) from a reserve fund for a sudden income loss, until moving to deferral.
